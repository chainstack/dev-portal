**Multichain API**. You can now use one auth token across the [Global Nodes](/docs/global-elastic-node) and [Dedicated Nodes](/docs/dedicated-node) you attach to a multichain endpoint, instead of one auth token per node. Available on every plan, including the Developer plan. See [Use Multichain API](/docs/multichain-api).

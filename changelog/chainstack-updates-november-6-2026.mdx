**Base**. Base's Denim upgrade activated on Base Mainnet on Nov 5, 2026, at 18:00 UTC: Base now produces a canonical block every 200 ms instead of every 2 seconds, and Flashblocks are retired. Replace Flashblocks subscriptions and `pending`-tag reads with `newHeads`, `logs`, and `latest`, and check any limit or logic that counts blocks. See [Base Denim: 200 ms blocks](/docs/base-denim-200ms-blocks) and [Stream real-time transactions on Base](/docs/base-streaming-transactions).

---

**Starknet**. Chainstack upgraded Starknet Mainnet and Sepolia nodes to [Pathfinder v0.24.2](https://github.com/software-mansion/pathfinder/releases/tag/v0.24.2). [`starknet_estimateMessageFee`](/reference/starknet-starknetestimatemessagefee) now returns an `overall_fee` of at least 50,000,000,000,000 wei (0.00005 ETH), the minimum fee for L1 to L2 messages. When the minimum applies, `overall_fee` is higher than the sum of the gas amounts multiplied by their prices.

**Node credential rotation**. You can now rotate a node's credentials on Chainstack without downtime. Generate new credentials, update your applications while the old and new credentials both work, and then deactivate the old ones. A rotation replaces the node's auth token, username and password, and gRPC x-token together. See [Rotate node credentials](/docs/manage-your-node#rotate-node-credentials).

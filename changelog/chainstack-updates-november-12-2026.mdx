**Ethereum**. On Ethereum Hoodi [Global Nodes](/docs/global-elastic-node), the `safe` block tag now follows the Fast Confirmation Rule and typically trails `latest` by one or two blocks, so you can try fast confirmations on a testnet. `finalized` is unchanged. See [Ethereum: fast confirmation and the safe block tag](/docs/ethereum-fast-confirmation).

**Ethereum**. On Ethereum Hoodi [Global Nodes](/docs/global-elastic-node), the `safe` block tag now follows the Fast Confirmation Rule and trails `latest` by a few blocks instead of a justified block dozens of blocks back. `finalized` is unchanged. See [Ethereum: fast confirmation and the safe block tag](/docs/ethereum-fast-confirmation).
